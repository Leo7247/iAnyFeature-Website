Help & Tutorials

Everything you need to know to install and maintain iAnyFeature apps.

1. AltStore Initial Setup (Requires Computer)

To install our apps without jailbreaking, you first need AltStore. You will need a Windows or Mac computer for this initial step.

Windows Users: You MUST download iTunes and iCloud directly from Apple's website. If you installed them from the Microsoft Store, uninstall them and download the direct installers.

1

Download AltServer

Go to altstore.io on your computer and download the appropriate version for macOS or Windows.

2

Connect Your Device

Plug your iPhone or iPad into your computer. Make sure to tap "Trust this computer" on your device screen if prompted.

3

Install AltStore via AltServer

Open AltServer on your computer. Click the AltServer icon in your menu bar (Mac) or system tray (Windows), select "Install AltStore," and choose your device. Enter your Apple ID and password (used only to communicate with Apple's developer servers).

4

Trust the Developer

On your iOS device, go to Settings > General > VPN & Device Management. Tap your Apple ID email and select "Trust". AltStore is now ready to use!

2. Adding the iAnyFeature Repository

Once AltStore is installed, add our official repository to get automatic app updates directly on your device.

1

Open AltStore

Launch the AltStore app on your iPhone or iPad.

2

Navigate to Sources

Tap the Browse tab at the bottom, then tap the Sources button in the top left corner.

3

Add iAnyFeature

Tap the + icon, or simply visit our AltStore Source page on your device and tap the green "Add to AltStore" button. Our apps like LetsChat will now appear in your Browse tab.

3. Manual IPA Sideloading (Alternative)

If you prefer not to use our repository, or if you are using an alternative signing service like Sideloadly or TrollStore, you can install the IPAs manually.

1

Download the IPA

Visit the My Apps page directly from your Safari browser and download the `.ipa` file to your device's Files app.

2

Share to Signing App

Open the Files app, tap the downloaded `.ipa` file, tap the Share icon, and select AltStore (or your preferred signing app) from the share sheet to begin installation.

Troubleshooting & FAQ

Why do apps expire after 7 days?

If you are using a standard free Apple ID, Apple limits sideloaded apps to a 7-day lifespan. You must refresh them via AltStore before they expire. Make sure AltServer is running on your computer on the same Wi-Fi network, and AltStore will attempt to refresh them automatically in the background.

Error: "Could not find AltServer"

Ensure your computer is powered on, awake, and AltServer is running. Verify that your iOS device and computer are connected to the exact same Wi-Fi network. If issues persist, plug your device directly into the computer via USB to refresh.

What is the Respring Portal?

The Respring Portal is an experimental utility meant for developers and advanced users to quickly restart the iOS SpringBoard (home screen) without fully rebooting the device. It utilizes a WebKit rendering loop. Use with caution.

"Untrusted Enterprise Developer" Error

This occurs the first time you install an app via a new certificate. Go to Settings > General > VPN & Device Management, tap the certificate name, and choose "Trust".

How to Install IPA Files

Follow these full guides to sideload LetsChat onto your iOS device using either a Windows PC or a Mac.

/ Method 1: Using Sideloadly (Recommended)

1
Download Sideloadly

Go to sideloadly.io on your Windows or Mac computer and download the appropriate version. Install it and open the application.

2
Download the App

Go to the My Apps page on this website and download the LetsChat `.ipa` file to your computer.

3
Connect Your Device

Plug your iPhone/iPad into your computer using a USB cable. Make sure your device is unlocked and tap "Trust this Computer" if prompted.

4
Install the App

Drag and drop the `.ipa` file into the Sideloadly window. Enter your Apple ID in the provided field, and click the Start button. Wait for the status to say "Done".

5
Trust the Developer

On your iOS device, navigate to Settings > General > VPN & Device Management. Tap on your Apple ID email, and tap "Trust". You can now open LetsChat!

Note: Free Apple Developer accounts require you to re-sideload the app every 7 days.

Method 2: Using AltStore

If you already have AltServer installed on your PC/Mac and AltStore on your phone:

  • Open AltStore on your iOS device.
  • Go to the Sources tab.
  • Tap the + button and paste my AltStore link (found on the AltStore Source page).
  • Download and update LetsChat wirelessly directly from the AltStore app!

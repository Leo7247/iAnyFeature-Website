Comprehensive Installation Guide

Follow these in-depth guides to safely sideload LetsChat onto your iOS device using either a Windows PC or a Mac. No jailbreak is required for these methods.

Method 1: PC/Mac via Sideloadly (Recommended)

1
Download Sideloadly

Navigate to sideloadly.io on your computer. Download the correct version (Windows 64-bit/32-bit or macOS) and install it. Note: Windows users must install the web version of iTunes and iCloud, not the Microsoft Store versions.

2
Get the LetsChat IPA File

Download the official IPA directly to your computer here:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Leo7247/iAnyFeature/refs/heads/main/LetsChat%204.3.0%20(43).ipa

3
Connect and Trust Your Device

Plug your iPhone or iPad into your computer using a certified USB cable. Unlock your iOS device. A prompt will appear asking if you want to "Trust This Computer." Tap Trust and enter your device passcode.

4
Inject the IPA

Open Sideloadly. Drag and drop the downloaded LetsChat `.ipa` file into the large IPA icon on the left side of the Sideloadly window. Enter your Apple ID email into the designated field. Click the Start button at the bottom.

Note: Sideloadly securely communicates directly with Apple servers to sign the app. If prompted, enter your Apple ID password and 2FA code.

5
Verify the Profile on iOS

Once Sideloadly says "Done", the LetsChat app will appear on your home screen. Do not open it yet. Go to Settings > General > VPN & Device Management. Tap on your Apple ID email under "Developer App" and tap Trust.

iOS 16 or Above: Enable Developer Mode
If you are on iOS 16 or above, you must enable Developer Mode before the app will launch. Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Developer Mode, toggle it ON, and restart your iPhone when prompted.

Method 2: Wireless AltStore Installation

If you already have AltServer running on your computer and AltStore installed on your device, this is the easiest way to keep LetsChat updated automatically.

1
Ensure Server Connection

Make sure AltServer is currently running in the background on your PC/Mac and your phone is connected to the exact same Wi-Fi network. (Mac users: ensure the AltPlugin is enabled in Mail Preferences).

2
Add the Repository

Open the AltStore app on your iPhone. Navigate to the Sources tab at the top. Tap the + button and paste my official source link, or simply click the "Add Source" button on the My Apps page of this website.

Install & Update

Once the source is added, LetsChat will appear in your Browse tab. Tap "Free" to install. AltStore will automatically refresh LetsChat in the background as long as it connects to your PC every 7 days.

The 7-Day Limit (For Non-Paid Apple IDs):
Because Apple restricts free developer accounts, sideloaded apps expire after 7 days. Sideloadly can automatically refresh apps via Wi-Fi if enabled in its settings, and AltStore refreshes them in the background automatically. If LetsChat crashes on launch after a week, simply re-sideload it using the steps above without deleting the app—your data will be saved.
